Every Setllend loan is over-collateralized. A borrower locks collateral tokens in the contract, and two numbers derived from that collateral govern the loan: **max LTV** limits how much the borrower can owe, and the **health factor** says when the loan can be [liquidated](./liquidation.mdx). Both are checked against the **debt face**, the principal plus all the fixed interest to maturity, not just the principal.

## Collateral tokens

The contract owner keeps a list of accepted collateral tokens, each with its own oracle feed and risk parameters. The list today:

| Token | Intents asset ID | Decimals | Max LTV | Liquidation threshold | Liquidation bonus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ETH (from Arbitrum) | `nep141:arb.omft.near` | 18 | 70% | 80% | 5% |
| ETH (from Base) | `nep141:base.omft.near` | 18 | 70% | 80% | 5% |
| NEAR (wrapped) | `nep141:wrap.near` | 24 | 70% | 80% | 5% |

Both ETH tokens are priced with the same ETH/USD feed. The full parameter list, and how to read it from the contract yourself, is on [Risk parameters](../reference/risk-parameters.md).

## One loan per maturity

A borrower has at most one loan per [maturity](./maturities.md). The loan holds every collateral token the borrower put into it, and its debt face is the sum of all their `borrow` fills in that maturity. Loans in different maturities are separate: collateral in one never backs debt in another.

Tokens you deposit into your Intents balance are **not** collateral yet. They become collateral when you escrow them in a `borrow` order and that order fills, or when you move them into a loan that has debt with `add_collateral`.

## Two checks: max LTV and liquidation threshold

**Max LTV** (loan to value) is the borrowing limit. A `borrow` fill, or a collateral withdrawal while the loan has debt, is allowed only if afterwards:

```
Σ collateral_value × max_ltv  ≥  debt_value          (tokens that aren't frozen)
```

**Liquidation threshold** is the point where the loan can be liquidated. The **health factor** is:

```
health_factor = Σ collateral_value × liq_threshold / debt_value     (every token)
```

A loan with debt is liquidatable when its health factor is **below 1**, or when it is still unpaid one hour after maturity (see [Liquidation](./liquidation.mdx)). The 10-point gap between 70% and 80% is the room a loan has before it can be liquidated: from a loan opened at exactly max LTV, collateral must lose 12.5% of its value before the health factor reaches 1.

Because both checks use the debt face, max LTV is slightly stricter than it looks: the interest to maturity counts against the limit from the start.

## Conservative prices

Prices come from the [oracle](./oracle.md) as a price and a confidence interval `conf`, an estimate of how far the true price may be from the reported one. For both checks the contract assumes the worse end of the range:

```
collateral_value = amount × (price − conf)          for each token
debt_value       = debt_face × (USDC price + conf)
```

So the numbers the contract uses are a little lower for collateral and a little higher for debt than the prices you see quoted. With ETH at $2,260.00 and a conf of $2.50 (about 0.11%, a typical value), and USDC at $1.0000 with a conf of $0.0014, 0.5 ETH against 500 USDC of debt face has a health factor of:

- at mid prices: 0.5 × 2,260 × 0.80 / 500 = **1.808**
- at conservative prices: 0.5 × 2,257.50 × 0.80 / (500 × 1.0014) = **1.803**

The contract compares these sums exactly, as integers, with no rounding. The health factor the app shows is computed the same conservative way from the same oracle prices, so a value below 1.00 means the loan can be liquidated at those prices. Without debt, a loan has no health factor.

## When each check runs

| Action | Price needed | Check |
|---|---|---|
| A `borrow` fill | Yes | The whole loan, after the fill, meets max LTV. Otherwise the fill is skipped (`cannot_open`) |
| Withdraw collateral, loan has debt | Yes | The loan, after the withdrawal, meets max LTV, and it is before maturity + 1 hour. Otherwise `Withdrawal exceeds max LTV` or `Debt is overdue` |
| Withdraw collateral, loan has no debt | No | None; the collateral is paid out at once |
| Add collateral | No | The loan must have debt, and it must be before maturity + 1 hour. It only raises health |
| Repay | No | None |
| Liquidate | Yes | Health factor below 1, or unpaid at maturity + 1 hour |

## Frozen tokens

The owner can **freeze** a collateral token, for example if its market or bridge becomes unsafe. A frozen token:

- can't be escrowed in a new `borrow` order or added to a loan;
- gives **no borrowing power**: the max LTV check leaves it out, so it can't support new borrowing or a withdrawal of other collateral;
- still **counts toward health** at its liquidation threshold, so a freeze never makes a loan liquidatable by itself.

Open loans that hold a frozen token run on until they are repaid, withdrawn, or liquidated. This is how Aave v3 freezes a reserve.

## Worked example

Borrow **1,000 USDC for 21 days at 8% APR**, with ETH at $2,260:

1. Debt face = 1,000 × (1 + 0.08 × 21 / 365) ≈ **1,004.60 USDC**.
2. Minimum collateral at 70% max LTV = 1,004.60 / 0.70 ≈ $1,435.15, which is about **0.635 ETH**, plus a little for the price's conf.

The other way round, with **0.05 ETH** ($113) as collateral, the most the loan can owe is 113 × 0.70 = $79.10 of debt face, which at the same terms is a principal of about **78.74 USDC**.

A loan with 70 USDC of debt face on 0.05 ETH has a health factor of 0.05 × 2,260 × 0.80 / 70 ≈ **1.29**. It reaches 1 when 0.05 ETH is worth 70 / 0.80 = $87.50, which is ETH at about **$1,750**, a drop of about 22.6%. The [Liquidation](./liquidation.mdx) page follows this loan through a liquidation.

**Several tokens.** A loan with 0.5 ETH at $2,260 ($1,130) and 200 NEAR at $2.50 ($500) holds $1,630 of collateral. Its borrowing power is 1,130 × 0.70 + 500 × 0.70 = $1,141 of debt face. With 900 USDC of debt face its health factor is (1,130 × 0.80 + 500 × 0.80) / 900 ≈ **1.45**.

:::warning The owner can change parameters at once
The contract owner can change a token's max LTV, liquidation threshold, and liquidation bonus at any time, and the change applies immediately to open loans too. Lowering a threshold can make a loan liquidatable without warning. Keep a margin above a health factor of 1.
:::

## Keeping a loan healthy

- **Add collateral** to the loan at any time while it has debt, up to maturity + 1 hour.
- **Repay** part of the debt, or buy notes back in the same maturity so they [net](./zero-coupon-notes.md#netting) against it.
- **Watch the health factor** in Portfolio, especially when ETH or NEAR moves fast.
